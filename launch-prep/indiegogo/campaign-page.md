## Meet Mess Monster

Mess Monster is a zero-shame cleaning motivation app for Android. Instead of guilt-tripping you with overdue tasks and punishing streaks, it gives you a virtual pet whose whole world responds to your real-world cleaning.

Log a task, earn points, buy your monster a snack. Keep going and it glows. Miss a few days and it gets a little sad — but it never loses progress, never punishes you, and never judges you for a rough week.

**It just waits. And the moment you come back, it bounces right back too.**

---

## How It Works

[GRAPHIC: Simple loop — Clean → Log → Earn → Care → Monster thrives → repeat]

1. **Clean something** — pick from 6 daily rotating tasks (dishes, counters, vacuuming, bathroom, trash, and more)
2. **Log it in the app** — tap to complete; optional photo verification for bonus rewards
3. **Earn points** — base rewards, bonus multipliers, and rare jackpots
4. **Spend points in the Monster Store** — food, toys, accessories, room decor
5. **Watch your monster thrive** — health and happiness bars rise; keep cleaning and see what happens

No calendar. No overdue count. No red alerts.

## Zero Shame. Always.

**What Mess Monster will never do:**

- ❌ Show you a wall of "OVERDUE" tasks
- ❌ Scold you for missing a day
- ❌ De-evolve your monster as punishment
- ❌ Make your monster angry at you
- ❌ Guilt-trip you back into the app

**What it does instead:**

- ✅ Your monster gets a little sad when you've been away — then bounces right back the moment you return
- ✅ Streak milestones reward you at 3, 7, 14, and 30 days — bonus points, no penalty for missing
- ✅ Six fresh tasks every day — no overdue count, no calendar pressure
- ✅ Optional photo verification for surprise bonus rewards (never required)
- ✅ All data stays on your device — no account, no sign-up friction, no cloud

## Built for Real Human Brains

Mess Monster was designed with ADHD, executive dysfunction, and cleaning anxiety in mind — because that's exactly who uses it.

The science is simple: shame spirals make cleaning harder. When you feel bad about your mess, you freeze. When you feel good about a small win — washing one cup, wiping one counter — you want to do more.

Mess Monster is built around small wins. One task. One point. One happier monster. That's the whole game.

Premium unlocks Spotify cleaning playlist integration, push notification reminders, and future premium content. The core loop — tasks, points, monster care, the store, streaks, photo verification — is free forever. No features that exist today will ever move behind a paywall.

**What platform is this?**
Android only. iOS is not planned at this time.

**Will my monster die if I stop using the app?**
No. Your monster's health and happiness bars drop and it'll look sad — but it never permanently loses progress, never de-evolves, and never disappears. Come back whenever you're ready.
