Your mess has finally met its match.

Mess Monster is the zero-shame cleaning motivator that turns real-world tidying into fuel for an adorable virtual pet — one that thrives when you clean and gets a little sad when life gets in the way. No judgment. No punishment. Just a creature that needs you, and a home that's getting cleaner.

Log a cleaning task → earn points → spend points to care for your monster. Your monster's health and happiness respond to your habits in real-time. Keep it up and it glows with life. Skip a few days and it droops a little — but it never permanently loses progress, never de-evolves, and never guilt-trips you. Come back whenever you're ready.

🌿 Nilly — mint-green kawaii cutie with sparkly leaf energy. Sweet, soft, wholesome.

DAILY TASKS THAT FIT REAL LIFE

• 6 fresh cleaning tasks every day, rotated automatically
• Covers Kitchen, Bathroom, Bedroom, Living Room, Laundry, Trash & more
• Optional photo verification: snap your clean space for a chance at bonus points or a surprise gift
• Rare jackpot rewards for cleaning overachievers
• No overdue alerts. No red warnings. No shame.

STREAKS & MILESTONES

Build a streak and earn bonus points at 3, 7, 14, and 30 consecutive days. Miss a day? No drama. Every time you come back counts.
