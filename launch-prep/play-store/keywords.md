## Strategic Notes

**"Zero-Shame Cleaning" is our differentiator keyword.** No competitor in the research dataset ranks for or owns this language. It should anchor taglines, ad copy, and social content — not just ASO metadata.

**ADHD positioning is a strong converter.** Across the competitor review data, "helps with ADHD / executive dysfunction" appears in the top praise themes for Sweepy, FlyLady, Habitica, RoutineFlow, and Finch. The audience is underserved and vocal. Lead with it in screenshots and feature bullets.

**"Chore Chart" leans family-focused in search intent.** Use it in secondary copy (feature bullets, screenshot captions) rather than as the headline keyword — it may attract users expecting a shared household tool rather than a solo pet game.

**Avoid "Virtual Tamagotchi" / "Tamagotchi App"** — Bandai Namco's trademarks and app (My Tamagotchi Forever, 10M+ downloads) make this a legal and ASO risk.

**Peridot (Niantic AR pet) is discontinued as of April 2026** — this keyword niche has freed up. "AR pet game" could be worth monitoring, though Mess Monster isn't AR.
