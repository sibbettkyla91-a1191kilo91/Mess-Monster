# Privacy Policy — Mess Monster

**Effective Date:** June 11, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 11, 2026

Mess Monster ("the App," "we," "us") is a gamified cleaning motivation app for Android. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

---

## 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All core App data is stored exclusively on your device using AsyncStorage and is never transmitted to any external server:

- Your monster's name, type (Nilly or Luna), and progression state
- Task history, daily streaks, and earned points
- Monster store purchases and cosmetic selections
- Optional: Task verification photos (stored locally, never uploaded)

---

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

- No personal information (name, email address, phone number, or physical address)
- No account creation or login required
- No device identifiers (IDFA, GAID, or similar advertising IDs)
- No location data
- No analytics or behavioral tracking
- No crash reporting or telemetry
- No data is ever sent to our servers — we don't have any

---

## 3. Google Play Billing (Premium Subscription)

If you subscribe to Mess Monster Premium, your payment is processed entirely by Google LLC via Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card information. We receive only a confirmation of your active subscription status from Google Play.

Google's privacy policy governs their handling of payment data:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 4. Spotify Integration (Premium Feature)

If you choose to connect your Spotify account as a premium feature, the App uses Spotify's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. We request read-only access to your playback state solely to surface now-playing information within the App. We do not store your Spotify credentials, refresh token, or listening history beyond your active session.

Spotify's privacy policy governs their handling of your account data:
https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/

---

## 5. Push Notifications

If you choose to enable push notifications, a notification token (a non-personally-identifiable device identifier) is used solely to deliver cleaning reminders and monster mood alerts to your device. Notification tokens are not shared with any third party and are removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the App.

---

## 6. Camera Access

The App may request access to your device camera for the optional task photo-verification feature. Photos are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party. You can use the App fully without granting camera access.

---

## 7. Haptic Feedback

The App uses device haptics for interactive feedback (e.g., tab presses). No data is collected through haptic events.

---

## 8. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with any third parties, except as described in Sections 3 and 4 above (Google Play for billing confirmation, Spotify for playback state). Neither of these services receives your monster data, task history, or cleaning behavior.

---

## 9. Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all associated App data. There is no server-side copy, so no data deletion request to us is necessary.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the App and you have concerns, please contact us.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example, when new features are added). Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
