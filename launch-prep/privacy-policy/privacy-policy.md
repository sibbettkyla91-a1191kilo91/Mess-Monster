# Privacy Policy — Mess Monster

**Effective Date:** June 11, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

Mess Monster ("the App," "we," "us") is a gamified cleaning motivation app for Android. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the App uses and what it does not.

The App is free to download and use. There is no checkout in this version. A later "Founding Member" extra may be added someday; it is not for sale yet.

---

## 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All core App data stays on your device. We do not have an account system, and we do not run a Mess Monster server that stores your progress.

On your device, the App remembers:

- Your monster's name, type (Nilly or Luna), and how they are doing
- Task history, daily streaks, and earned points
- Shop items you bought with in-app points (not real money)
- Optional task photos, if you choose to take them

Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

---

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

- No name, email, phone number, or home address
- No account or login
- No advertising IDs
- No location
- No analytics or marketing trackers
- No payment card details (there is no real-money purchase yet)

---

## 3. Crash Reports (optional, off unless we turn them on)

The App can send crash reports to Sentry so we can fix bugs. That only happens if we add a Sentry key to a future build. The current Play test build does not include that key, so no crash reports are sent.

If we turn this on later, reports may include the type of crash and technical device info. They will not include your monster name, chores, or photos. We will update this policy if that changes.

---

## 4. Founding Member and future payments

Founding Member is shown as "Coming soon." We do not take real money in this version. If we add Google Play billing later, Google will handle the payment. We would only learn that a purchase succeeded — not your card number.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 5. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules gentle local reminders on your phone (for example, a evening check-in). These do not use a remote push service and are not shared with anyone. You can say no and still use the App.

---

## 6. Camera Access

The App may ask for the camera so you can optionally snap a cleaned space for a bonus. Photos stay on your device. They are never uploaded. You can skip photos and still finish chores.

---

## 7. Haptic Feedback

The App may vibrate on taps. No data is collected from that.

---

## 8. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not share your monster, chores, or photos with anyone.

---

## 9. Data Retention & Deletion

Everything lives on your device. Uninstall the App to delete it. There is no copy on our servers, so you do not need to email us to wipe a cloud account.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date. Keep using the App after that means you are okay with the new version.
