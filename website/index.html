The Idea

We know how it feels. The laundry pile is staring you down, the dishes are mounting, and your brain just... won't. When you have ADHD or struggle with executive dysfunction, "just doing it" isn't a thing. It's a wall.

Mess Monster changes the game. You're not cleaning for yourself anymore. You're cleaning for your monster. When you finish a task, they get stronger, happier, and evolve. When the mess takes over, they need your help to get back on their feet. It's zero-shame, zero-judgment—just you and your best friend, tackling the chaos together.

Meet the Monsters

Choose your monster at launch — your choice sets the whole app's color theme.

Nilly

The Sweet Encourager

Nilly is a round, fluffy, mint-green ball of pure positivity. They love soft blankets and the smell of fresh laundry. Nilly will never judge you for a bad day; they'll just be there waiting with a smile when you're ready to try again.

Luna

The Mysterious Guardian

Luna is a sleek black monster with glowing orange eyes and golden horns. Wearing a classic witch hat, Luna brings a cool, magical energy to your cleaning routine. They appreciate the quiet of a tidy room and the sparkle of a job well done.

About the Developer

I built Mess Monster because I couldn't get motivated to clean. No app I tried made me actually want to start — they just made me feel guilty for not finishing. As someone who knows firsthand how ADHD and executive dysfunction can make even small tasks feel impossible, I wanted something different. So I did what any reasonable person would do: I invented two little monsters who need me. Turns out cleaning for creatures that love you hits different. That's Mess Monster.
